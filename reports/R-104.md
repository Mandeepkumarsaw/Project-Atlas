## 1. Executive Summary & Market Scope

Commercial building energy management systems (BEMS) integrate Internet of Things (IoT) sensors, automated HVAC controls, smart lighting, and cloud-based predictive energy software to monitor and optimize commercial facility power draw.

### Scope of Analysis
- **Software Platforms:** Energy information systems (EIS), fault detection and diagnostics (FDD), and continuous commissioning cloud dashboards.
- **Hardware & Automation:** BACnet/IP edge controllers, smart thermostats, programmable sub-meters, and motorized airflow dampers.
- **Enterprise Services:** Measurement and verification (M&V), ESG carbon reporting compliance, and automated peak demand response.
