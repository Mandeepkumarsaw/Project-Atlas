## 1. Executive Summary & Market Scope

Heavy industrial operations (power generation, semiconductor manufacturing, petrochemical refining, food & beverage, and mining) rely on specialized industrial water treatment equipment to purify raw intake water and treat industrial wastewater for reuse.

### Scope of Equipment Covered
- **Membrane Systems:** Ultra-high pressure reverse osmosis, nanofiltration, electrodialysis reversal (EDR).
- **Thermal & Evaporative Equipment:** Mechanical vapor recompression (MVR), crystallizers, multi-stage flash distillation for Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD).
- **Disinfection & Separation:** Ozone generators, industrial ultraviolet systems, dissolved air flotation (DAF), and clarification solids contact units.
