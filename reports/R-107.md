## 1. Executive Summary & Market Scope

The modern industrial manufacturing sector is experiencing rapid digital transformation driven by the deployment of Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) sensors, industrial edge computing gateways, and cloud-native predictive asset analytics.

### Scope of Analysis
- **Connected Edge Devices:** Vibration transmitters, acoustic leak detectors, thermal imaging sensors, and industrial smart meters deployed on plant machinery.
- **Communications & Gateways:** Cellular 5G private networks, IO-Link masters, and ruggedized edge compute servers running localized inference.
- **Predictive Maintenance & Analytics Software:** Cloud platforms executing remaining useful life (RUL) estimation, anomalous vibration detection, and automated maintenance work-order generation.
