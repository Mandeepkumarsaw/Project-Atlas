## 1. Executive Summary & Market Scope

Electric distribution and transmission utilities are adopting artificial intelligence and advanced spatial analytics to manage intermittent distributed energy resources (DERs), electric vehicle (EV) fleet charging loads, and catastrophic grid wildfire risks.

### Scope of Analysis
- **Grid Analytics Software Solutions:** Advanced Distribution Management System (ADMS) analytics, Distributed Energy Resource Management Systems (DERMS), asset health indexing, and short-term load forecasting.
- **Data Ingestion Engines:** High-frequency synchrophasor PMU analytics, AMI interval data processing, and drone/satellite visual inspection AI.
- **Utility Deployments:** Investor-Owned Utilities (IOUs), municipal electricity co-operatives, and transmission system operators (TSOs).
