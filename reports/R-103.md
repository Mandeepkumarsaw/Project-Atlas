## 1. Executive Summary & Market Definition

The global residential water purifier industry is experiencing accelerated transformation fueled by heightened awareness of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS / "forever chemicals"), microplastic contamination in municipal tap water, and the rising penetration of smart IoT-enabled point-of-use systems.

### Scope & Product Categorization
This report covers point-of-use (POU) and point-of-entry (POE) water purification appliances purchased for domestic residential use:
- **Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems:** Multi-stage filtration incorporating semi-permeable membranes, mineralization cartridges, and automated flushing.
- **Ultraviolet (UV) & Ultrafiltration (UF) Systems:** Chemical-free disinfection targeting microbial pathogens without wastewater discharge.
- **Gravity-based and Pitcher Filters:** Low-cost entry filtration for regions with variable municipal grid pressure.
- **Smart Countertop & Under-Sink Dispensers:** Direct chill/boil filtration systems with mobile app filter life telemetry.

## 2. Global Market Valuation & Growth Forecast

In 2024, the global residential water purifier market reached a valuation of **USD 12.45 billion**.

### Growth Projections (2025–2030)
- **2024 Base Year Valuation:** **USD 12.45 billion**
- **Forecast Period:** 2025 to 2030
- **Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR):** **8.4%**
- **Projected 2030 Market Size:** **USD 18.64 billion**

### Core Growth Drivers
1. **Contaminant Consciousness:** New EPA national primary drinking water regulations imposing maximum contaminant levels (MCLs) for six PFAS compounds.
2. **Subscription Replacement Filters:** Recurring cartridge-as-a-service direct-to-door delivery models generating high-margin aftermarket revenue.
3. **Wastewater Reduction Innovation:** 1:1 and zero-waste RO membrane technologies addressing water conservation regulations in water-stressed urban zones.
